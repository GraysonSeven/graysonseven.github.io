THE PROBLEM

WHY IT
EXISTS.

Store operations become fragile when sales, inventory, purchasing, fulfillment and reporting live in separate workflows. Trade Core was designed as one operational core instead of a collection of disconnected tools.

The product grew around transaction-first store workflows, local-first reliability, role-specific interfaces, controlled cloud synchronization, thermal printing, backup/restore and owner-facing intelligence.
