4. Four-proof lead standard

[ ] Company proof: the account actually fits the ICP, including geography and size.
[ ] Role proof: the person has plausible responsibility or influence.
[ ] Contact proof: there is a legitimate route to reach them.
[ ] Reason proof: there is a concrete account-specific reason the account is relevant.
Assessed completion: use a fresh 10-lead batch you researched yourself. Do not copy demonstration or pilot leads. Conflicting evidence must be resolved or the lead must be rejected.
