QUESTIONS // BEFORE YOU CONTACT ME

THE THINGS
YOU NEED TO KNOW.

Cost, timeline and technical choices depend on the actual scope, so they are discussed after the project is understood.

How much does a project cost?

There is no single fixed price for every project. A simple site and a custom operational system are completely different jobs. I review what you actually need first, then the scope and price are agreed before payment.

How long will it take?

The timeline depends on the size, functionality and amount of revision involved. I would rather give you a timeline based on the real scope than advertise a number that may not apply to your project.

Do I need to already have a website design?

No. For website projects, the Website Studio lets you describe what you need and either let me choose the design direction or customize the visual direction yourself.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. A redesign can focus on clarity, visual identity, mobile usability, navigation, performance or the way visitors are guided toward the next action.

Can you build something more complex than a website?

Yes. My documented work includes web and Android applications, business workflows, local and cloud data, operational systems and product development. The exact fit depends on the project.

What happens before I pay?

Sending a website request is free and does not commit you to buying. I review the request, and scope and price are made clear before payment. The Website Studio also explains its website-specific preview and release flow.

Does “SEO-ready” mean you guarantee Google rankings?

No. I can build a technically sound SEO foundation — structure, metadata, mobile usability and crawlable pages — but no developer can honestly guarantee a specific search ranking.

Can special functionality change the price?

Yes. Custom functionality, larger scope and more complex integrations can change the total project price. That is why those requirements are identified before the final scope is agreed.

Can I contact you without using the Website Studio?

Yes. The Studio is simply the easiest guided path for website requests. You can also email, call or contact me through Facebook from the Contact page.
